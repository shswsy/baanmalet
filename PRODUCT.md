## Platform

web

## Users

Visitors to a Chiang Mai café and roastery: locals and tourists, Thai and English speakers, mostly on mobile. They are checking the menu, prices, hours and location, then contacting the shop on LINE. A Fastwork buyer evaluating the studio is the secondary audience.

## Product Purpose

A marketing landing page for บ้านเมล็ด (baanmalet), a **fictional** café and roastery. It shows the studio can build a fast, bilingual small-business site. Success means a visitor finds what they need and taps through to contact.

## Operating Context

- LINE buttons open a demo dialog with the prefilled message. There is no real LINE OA.
